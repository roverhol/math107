A — Definitions (key points)

A1. \(\mathbf{A}\) has \(m\) rows and \(n\) columns; each column is an \(m\)-vector.

A2. \(A_{ij}\) is the entry in row \(i\), column \(j\); \(\mathbf{a}_j\) is the \(j\)th column of \(\mathbf{A}\). Here \(A_{12}=-1\) and \(\mathbf{a}_2=\boldsymbol{\begin{bmatrix}-1\\ 2\end{bmatrix}}\).

A3. \(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}=x_1 \mathbf{a}_1+\cdots+x_n \mathbf{a}_n\).

A4. Input \(\mathbf{x}\), output \(\mathbf{y}\), transform \(\mathbf{A}\). Size of \(\mathbf{A}\) is \(m\times n\).

A5. \(\mathbf{A}\): triangular. \(\mathbf{B}\): identity, diagonal, triangular, symmetric. \(\mathbf{C}\): sparse.

A6. \(\mathbf{b}\in C(\mathbf{A})\) (equivalently: \(\mathbf{b}\) is a linear combination of the columns).
