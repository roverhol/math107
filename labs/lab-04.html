Learning objectives

  1. Compute a matrix inverse in software and check \(A^{-1}A=I\).
  2. Recognize failure of inv / a singular matrix, and connect that to a nontrivial null space.
  3. Read rank and nullity (\(\dim N(A)=n-\operatorname{rank}(A)\)) from software or RREF ideas.
  4. Compute determinants and interpret \(\det A\neq 0\) vs \(\det A=0\).

Math connections

Lecture 24 and Lecture 25. Good Midterm 2 practice for inverses, rank, and determinants.

Key syntax (this lab)

Task Octave Python
Inverse inv(A) np.linalg.inv(A)
Determinant det(A) np.linalg.det(A)
Rank rank(A) np.linalg.matrix_rank(A)

Ops strip — an invertible \(2\times 2\)

Use the Lecture 24 example \[ A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\2&3\end{bmatrix}. \]

C4.0. Enter \(A\). Compute \(A^{-1}\) in software. Print \(A^{-1}A\) (or \(AA^{-1}\)). What should you see?

Expected: \(A^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}3&-1\\-2&1\end{bmatrix}\); product is (numerically) \(I\).

C4.1. Check against the \(2\times 2\) formula \[ A^{-1}=\frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{bmatrix}. \] Does software match? What is \(\det A\)?

Expected: \(\det A=1\); formula matches C4.0.

Application — singular matrix, rank–nullity, determinants

C4.2. Let \[ B=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix} \] (Lecture 24’s non-invertible example). Try inv(B). What happens (error, warning, or huge numbers)?

Expected: failure / singular warning — \(B\) has no inverse.

C4.3. For the same \(B\): find \(\operatorname{rank}(B)\) and a nonzero vector in \(N(B)\) (hand or software). How many free variables? Check rank–nullity: \(\dim N(B)=2-\operatorname{rank}(B)\).

Expected: \(\operatorname{rank}(B)=1\), nullity \(1\), e.g. \((2,-1)^T\) (or \((-2,1)^T\)) in \(N(B)\).

C4.4. Determinants and meaning.

  1. Compute \(\det A\) and \(\det B\) (hand or software).
  2. One sentence each: what does \(\det A\neq 0\) tell you? What does \(\det B=0\) tell you?
  3. (Optional stretch.) \(\det\begin{bmatrix}2&0&0\\1&-3&0\\4&5&1\end{bmatrix}\) — use the triangular shortcut.

Expected: (a) \(\det A=1\), \(\det B=0\). (b) \(A\) invertible / full area scale; \(B\) singular, image of the unit square has area \(0\), no \(B^{-1}\). (c) product of diagonal \(=2\cdot(-3)\cdot 1=-6\).
