Ops strip (2–3 min)

Three song feature vectors (loudness, tempo) — units already scaled for today: \[ a=(1,2),\quad b=(2,1),\quad c=(4,3). \]

C1.0. Enter a, b, c. Print each and the length of a.

Expected: length \(2\).

Application (~15 min)

C1.1. Compute \(\lVert a-b\rVert\) and \(\lVert a-c\rVert\) (Euclidean).

Expected: \(\lVert a-b\rVert=\sqrt{2}\approx 1.414\); \(\lVert a-c\rVert=\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt{2}\approx 4.243\).

Octave: norm(a-b)
Python: np.linalg.norm(a-b)

C1.2. Which of \(b\) or \(c\) is the nearest neighbor of query \(a\)? One sentence: what does that mean if these were song features?

Expected: \(b\) is nearer; among these two songs, \(b\) is more similar to \(a\) in (loudness, tempo).

C1.3 (scale warning). Replace \(c\) by \(c_{\mathrm{bad}}=(4,30)\) (tempo mistyped \(\times 10\)). Recompute \(\lVert a-c_{\mathrm{bad}}\rVert\). Does the nearest neighbor of \(a\) among \(\{b,c_{\mathrm{bad}}\}\) change?

Expected: distance becomes large (\(\approx 28.16\)); NN is still \(b\), but the gap is dominated by the bad scale — feature units matter.

## Ops strip (2–3 min)

Three song feature vectors (loudness, tempo) — units already scaled for today:
$$
a=(1,2),\quad b=(2,1),\quad c=(4,3).
$$

**C1.0.** Enter `a`, `b`, `c`. Print each and the length of `a`.

**Expected:** length $2$.

## Application (~15 min)

**C1.1.** Compute $\lVert a-b\rVert$ and $\lVert a-c\rVert$ (Euclidean).

**Expected:** $\lVert a-b\rVert=\sqrt{2}\approx 1.414$; $\lVert a-c\rVert=\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt{2}\approx 4.243$.

**Octave:** `norm(a-b)`  
**Python:** `np.linalg.norm(a-b)`

**C1.2.** Which of $b$ or $c$ is the nearest neighbor of query $a$? One sentence: what does that mean if these were song features?

**Expected:** $b$ is nearer; among these two songs, $b$ is more similar to $a$ in (loudness, tempo).

**C1.3 (scale warning).** Replace $c$ by $c_{\mathrm{bad}}=(4,30)$ (tempo mistyped $\times 10$). Recompute $\lVert a-c_{\mathrm{bad}}\rVert$. Does the nearest neighbor of $a$ among $\{b,c_{\mathrm{bad}}\}$ change?

**Expected:** distance becomes large ($\approx 28.16$); NN is still $b$, but the gap is dominated by the bad scale — feature units matter.
