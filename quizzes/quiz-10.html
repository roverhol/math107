Friday, December 4 — eigenvalues, complex numbers, and Lab 5 (Week 13)

Covers the prior instructional week only: Eigenvectors, Characteristic, Eigenbehavior, and Lab 5 (Week 13). Fall break is skipped when choosing the prior week. Same-day SVD / Lab 6 / least squares (Week 15) are not on this quiz.

Textbooks: Strang §6.1 · Lachniet Octave PDF · also on Resources.

Schedule

SVD and least squares are not on this quiz; see Lab 6, the capstone packet, and the final.

Core

Fluency (complete prompts — practice these by hand)

C10.1. Let \[ A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad v=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}. \] Compute \(Av\). Is \(v\) an eigenvector? If so, what is \(\lambda\)?

C10.2. You are told that \(v=(2,-1)\) is an eigenvector of \[ A=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\1&3\end{bmatrix}. \] Find \(\lambda\) by comparing components of \(Av\) and \(v\). Then verify \(Av=\lambda v\).

C10.3. Let \(A=\operatorname{diag}(1.2,\,0.5)\) (Lab 4 / Lecture 35 mystery).

  1. Name two real eigenpairs using the standard basis.
  2. If \(x_0=(1,1)\), write \(x_t=A^t x_0\) componentwise. Which term dominates for large \(t\)?

C10.4. Complex drill. Let \(z=2+i\) and \(w=1-3i\). Compute \(z+w\), \(zw\), and \(|z|\).

C10.5. For \[ A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&3\end{bmatrix}, \] form \(\det(A-\lambda I)\), find both eigenvalues, and give one eigenvector for \(\lambda=3\).

C10.6. Rotation. Let \(R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}\) (\(90^\circ\) CCW).

  1. Show that the characteristic polynomial is \(\lambda^2+1=0\), so \(\lambda=\pm i\).
  2. Why does this match the geometric fact that \(R\) has no real eigenvector?

C10.7. Check a complex pair. With \(A=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}\) and \(v=(1,-i)\), compute \(Av\) and decide whether \(Av=iv\) or \(Av=(-i)v\).

C10.8. True or false, with a one-line reason: if \(Av=2v\) and \(v\neq 0\), then \(A^3 v=8v\).

C10.9 (Lab 5 / software). Suppose Octave/Python reports eigenvalues of a \(2\times 2\) matrix as approximately \(0.9\) and \(0.2\), with corresponding eigenvectors \(v_1\approx(0.7,\,0.7)\) and \(v_2\approx(-0.7,\,0.7)\) (normalized). For dynamics \(x_{t+1}=Ax_t\) started near \(v_1+v_2\), which mode dominates for large \(t\), and does the state grow or decay?

C10.10 (Lab 5 / verify). Software prints \(\lambda\approx 3\) and \(v\approx(0.707,\,0.707)\) for \[ A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}. \] Explain how you would verify \(Av\approx\lambda v\) numerically in one or two lines of code (Octave or Python). What should \(\|Av-\lambda v\|\) look like if the pair is correct?

What a quiz item might change

## Core

### Fluency (complete prompts — practice these by hand)

**C10.1.** Let
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
v=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Compute $Av$. Is $v$ an eigenvector? If so, what is $\lambda$?

**C10.2.** You are told that $v=(2,-1)$ is an eigenvector of
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\1&3\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Find $\lambda$ by comparing components of $Av$ and $v$. Then verify $Av=\lambda v$.

**C10.3.** Let $A=\operatorname{diag}(1.2,\,0.5)$ (Lab 4 / Lecture 35 mystery).

(a) Name two real eigenpairs using the standard basis.
(b) If $x_0=(1,1)$, write $x_t=A^t x_0$ componentwise. Which term dominates for large $t$?

**C10.4.** Complex drill. Let $z=2+i$ and $w=1-3i$. Compute $z+w$, $zw$, and $|z|$.

**C10.5.** For
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&3\end{bmatrix},
$$
form $\det(A-\lambda I)$, find both eigenvalues, and give one eigenvector for $\lambda=3$.

**C10.6.** Rotation. Let $R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ ($90^\circ$ CCW).

(a) Show that the characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2+1=0$, so $\lambda=\pm i$.
(b) Why does this match the geometric fact that $R$ has no real eigenvector?

**C10.7.** Check a complex pair. With $A=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ and $v=(1,-i)$, compute $Av$ and decide whether $Av=iv$ or $Av=(-i)v$.

**C10.8.** True or false, with a one-line reason: if $Av=2v$ and $v\neq 0$, then $A^3 v=8v$.

**C10.9 (Lab 5 / software).** Suppose Octave/Python reports eigenvalues of a $2\times 2$ matrix as approximately $0.9$ and $0.2$, with corresponding eigenvectors $v_1\approx(0.7,\,0.7)$ and $v_2\approx(-0.7,\,0.7)$ (normalized). For dynamics $x_{t+1}=Ax_t$ started near $v_1+v_2$, which mode dominates for large $t$, and does the state grow or decay?

**C10.10 (Lab 5 / verify).** Software prints $\lambda\approx 3$ and $v\approx(0.707,\,0.707)$ for
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Explain how you would verify $Av\approx\lambda v$ numerically in one or two lines of code (Octave or Python). What should $\|Av-\lambda v\|$ look like if the pair is correct?
