Textbook: Boyd & Vandenberghe, VMLS (PDF)

Covers: solving linear systems by elimination and elementary matrices (Lecture 21, Lecture 22, Lab 2 / RREF practice). Same-day powers and Lab 3 are not on this quiz.

Schedule

Elementary-matrix templates (also provided on the quiz paper) are at the end of this page.

Core

Part A — Augmented matrix, eliminate, check

For each system: (i) write the augmented matrix \([A\mid b]\); (ii) solve by elimination (row operations on the augmented matrix); (iii) check by substituting your solution into the original equations (or computing \(Ax\) and comparing to \(b\)).

C8.1. (\(2\times 2\)) \[ \begin{aligned} x_1 + 2x_2 &= 5 \\ 3x_1 - x_2 &= 1. \end{aligned} \]

C8.2. (\(3\times 2\) — three equations, two unknowns) \[ \begin{aligned} x_1 + x_2 &= 3 \\ 2x_1 - x_2 &= 0 \\ 3x_1 &= 3. \end{aligned} \]

C8.3. (\(2\times 3\) — two equations, three unknowns) \[ \begin{aligned} x_1 + 2x_2 + x_3 &= 4 \\ 2x_1 + 4x_2 + 2x_3 &= 8. \end{aligned} \] If there are infinitely many solutions, introduce a parameter \(t\) and write the general solution.

Part B — Name the elementary matrix

Use the templates at the end of the quiz (they will be provided). For each stated row operation on a system with the given number of equations, write the corresponding elementary matrix \(E\).

C8.4. Two equations: add \(-3\) times row 1 to row 2.

C8.5. Two equations: swap row 1 and row 2.

C8.6. Three equations: multiply row 2 by \(1/2\).

C8.7. Three equations: add \(2\) times row 1 to row 3.

Part C — Apply \(E\) and rewrite the system

C8.8. Let \[ [A\mid b]=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&|&3\\ 2&1&|&0\end{bmatrix}, \qquad E=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\ -2&1\end{bmatrix}. \] (a) Compute \(E\,[A\mid b]\) by hand.
(b) Rewrite the result as a system of equations.

C8.9. Let \[ [A\mid b]=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0&|&2\\ 0&1&1&|&3\\ 2&0&1&|&1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad E=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\ 0&1&0\\ -2&0&1\end{bmatrix}. \] (a) Compute \(E\,[A\mid b]\) by hand.
(b) Rewrite the result as a system of equations in \(x_1,x_2,x_3\).

Stretch

S8.1. Continue C8.8: from your new matrix, finish elimination and solve the \(2\times 2\) system. Check \(Ax=b\).

S8.2. For C8.3, write one particular solution (a specific numerical vector) and one nontrivial nullspace direction (homogeneous solution).

What a quiz item might change

The right-hand side, which row operation is named, or whether the system is \(2\times 2\), \(3\times 2\), or \(2\times 3\).

Elementary-matrix templates (provided on the quiz)

Left-multiplying \([A\mid b]\) by \(E\) performs the row operation. Build \(E\) by doing that same operation to the identity.

Two equations (\(2\times 2\) templates):

Swap the two rows: \[ \begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}. \]

Multiply row 1 by \(c\neq 0\), or multiply row 2 by \(c\neq 0\): \[ \begin{bmatrix}c&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&c\end{bmatrix}. \]

Add \(c\) times row 1 to row 2, or add \(c\) times row 2 to row 1: \[ \begin{bmatrix}1&0\\c&1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix}1&c\\0&1\end{bmatrix}. \]

Three equations: start from \(I_3\) and edit the same spot. Examples:

Multiply row 2 by \(c\neq 0\): \[ \begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&c&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}. \]

Add \(c\) times row 1 to row 3: \[ \begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\c&0&1\end{bmatrix}. \]

(The extra number sits in the row you change, in the column of the row you copy from.)

## Core

### Part A — Augmented matrix, eliminate, check

For each system: (i) write the augmented matrix $[A\mid b]$; (ii) solve by elimination (row operations on the augmented matrix); (iii) check by substituting your solution into the original equations (or computing $Ax$ and comparing to $b$).

**C8.1.** ($2\times 2$)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 + 2x_2 &= 5 \\
3x_1 - x_2 &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$

**C8.2.** ($3\times 2$ — three equations, two unknowns)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 + x_2 &= 3 \\
2x_1 - x_2 &= 0 \\
3x_1 &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$

**C8.3.** ($2\times 3$ — two equations, three unknowns)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 + 2x_2 + x_3 &= 4 \\
2x_1 + 4x_2 + 2x_3 &= 8.
\end{aligned}
$$
If there are infinitely many solutions, introduce a parameter $t$ and write the general solution.

### Part B — Name the elementary matrix

Use the templates at the end of the quiz (they will be provided). For each stated row operation on a system with the given number of equations, write the corresponding elementary matrix $E$.

**C8.4.** Two equations: add $-3$ times row 1 to row 2.

**C8.5.** Two equations: swap row 1 and row 2.

**C8.6.** Three equations: multiply row 2 by $1/2$.

**C8.7.** Three equations: add $2$ times row 1 to row 3.

### Part C — Apply $E$ and rewrite the system

**C8.8.** Let
$$
[A\mid b]=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&|&3\\ 2&1&|&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
E=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\ -2&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$
(a) Compute $E\,[A\mid b]$ by hand.  
(b) Rewrite the result as a system of equations.

**C8.9.** Let
$$
[A\mid b]=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0&|&2\\ 0&1&1&|&3\\ 2&0&1&|&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
E=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\ 0&1&0\\ -2&0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$
(a) Compute $E\,[A\mid b]$ by hand.  
(b) Rewrite the result as a system of equations in $x_1,x_2,x_3$.

## Stretch

**S8.1.** Continue C8.8: from your new matrix, finish elimination and solve the $2\times 2$ system. Check $Ax=b$.

**S8.2.** For C8.3, write one particular solution (a specific numerical vector) and one nontrivial nullspace direction (homogeneous solution).

## What a quiz item might change

The right-hand side, which row operation is named, or whether the system is $2\times 2$, $3\times 2$, or $2\times 3$.

## Elementary-matrix templates (provided on the quiz)

Left-multiplying $[A\mid b]$ by $E$ performs the row operation. Build $E$ by doing that same operation to the identity.

**Two equations** ($2\times 2$ templates):

Swap the two rows:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Multiply row 1 by $c\neq 0$, or multiply row 2 by $c\neq 0$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}c&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&c\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Add $c$ times row 1 to row 2, or add $c$ times row 2 to row 1:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\c&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}1&c\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**Three equations:** start from $I_3$ and edit the same spot. Examples:

Multiply row 2 by $c\neq 0$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&c&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Add $c$ times row 1 to row 3:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\c&0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

(The extra number sits in the row you change, in the column of the row you copy from.)
